# Composite-level decomposition: exact performance repair

This follows the integral morphism slice in PR #230. Its baseline benchmark
at levels $121,242,363$ was $4.8$–$5.9\times$ slower than Sage. The workload
constructs the labelled oldform copies, their product-to-Jacobian isogeny,
and its integral Smith invariants; it does not time only a rational splitting.

## Changes

### Saturation by the dual column lattice

Let $B$ be an $r\times n$ rational RREF basis. We need
$\operatorname{rowspan}_{\QQ}(B)\cap\ZZ^n$, not the unsaturated integer
span of denominator-cleared rows.

Let $L\subset\QQ^r$ be the lattice generated by the columns of $B$. The
unit pivot columns imply $\ZZ^r\subset L$. For a row vector $x$, the
condition $xB\in\ZZ^n$ is exactly $x\in L^*$. If the rows of $H$ form a
basis of $L$, the rows of $H^{-T}$ form a basis of $L^*$. Consequently
$H^{-T}B$ is an integral basis of the required saturated intersection.
One final integer HNF makes the output canonical.

Compute $H$ by clearing denominators of $B^T$ and taking HNF. Thus the
expensive operations have shapes $n\times r$ and $r\times r$, instead of
forming the $(n-r)\times n$ right kernel and then its kernel. At level
$363$, rank-$2$ and rank-$4$ constituents formerly produced matrices as
large as $87\times89$. Integral RREF bases need no saturation at all:
their unit pivot minor is already a certificate.

### Transfer by counting Manin generators

For an SL2 matrix $g$, its path $\{g(0),g(\infty)\}$ is the Manin
generator indexed by the projective bottom row. Choose independent
generators from the exact triple-Manin presentation. Count the translated
bottom rows for the covering cosets, then apply the presentation reduction
and the change to geometric E1 coordinates by matrix multiplication.

The E1 coordinate bridge is computed once per level and reused across
transfers. Public exceptional basis conventions are applied explicitly.
The covering-degree composition check is retained. No arbitrary-path
reduction backend, foreign binding, or handwritten native code is added.

### Avoid redundant geometry

- A same-level new constituent already is its embedded image; reuse it.
- Compute a saturated image without first constructing the nonsaturated
  image. An explicit `image_lattice()` request still returns the true
  integral image, with its independent cache and saturation status.
- Stack the factor inclusions to construct the product map. Its replayable
  certificate records each factor map and checks ordered endpoints. This
  avoids multiplying by large zero-filled projection matrices and adding
  a chain of intermediate maps. The public morphism model is unchanged.

## Qualification

The shared native/browser regression corpus checks:

- dual-column saturation against an independent double-kernel oracle,
  including dependent rows, zero ranks, coupled denominators and idempotence;
- transfers against explicit translated-path reductions, including the
  nonstandard public level-$11$ coordinate convention;
- product assembly against the explicit sum of inclusion/projection maps;
- original Sage Smith invariants, degeneracy cross-compositions, nonsaturated
  images, and authenticated serialization round trips and tampering.

Timing receipts must use fresh processes, the packaged mathematical sources,
and exact dimension/rank/Smith comparison with Sage. Include the original
three levels and larger composite levels $726$ and $1089$. Report any
remaining gap rather than marking universal performance parity from a bounded
benchmark. See `bench/modular/abelian-varieties/` for receipts.
