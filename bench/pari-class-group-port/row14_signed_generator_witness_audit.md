# Row-14 signed class-generator witness

The previous strict-v2 replay could authenticate the second published class
generator and its order relation, but could not reconstruct that ideal. Its
Smith quotient has negative entries:

```text
(-2, -1, -1) on factor-map rows 692, 767, 796
```

Authentication alone is weaker than the required statement that the
published ideal, raised to order eight, is generated by the stated exact
principal witness.

## Minimal material

No additional live PARI owner is required. The helper authenticates the
strict-v2 correspondence envelope and factor-metadata receipt before reading
their contents: compressed/plain authorities remain the checker's outer
boundary, while payload, source, metadata/core, capsule, and prepared-source
authorities are checked again at the mathematical helper boundary. The
immutable bundle already retains every source value needed by the translated
PARI 2.17.4 `genback` path:

- the 16-entry rounded T2 matrix from prepared-field metadata;
- the 64-entry integral-basis multiplication table;
- three 16-entry factor-base HNFs at rows 692, 767, and 796, whose primes and
  norms are respectively 5099, 5659, and 5939; and
- the independently derived Smith quotient `(-2, -1, -1)`.

The rounded T2 matrix and multiplication table are field owners, not answers.
The three source ideals are ordinary factor-base owners. The quotient is
already forced by the relation presentation and its order witness.

`row14_signed_generator_witness.py` computes the seven reduction candidates
from those inputs. It reconstructs the published second generator, in the
existing displayed column-HNF convention, and derives these seven ordered
principal-scaling factors:

## Exact equations

An independent fractional-HNF replay verifies

```text
G_1 * principal(Ge_1) = P_5099^-2 * P_5659^-1 * P_5939^-1.
```

The existing detached replay independently verifies all 806 raw equations

```text
principal(alpha_j) = product(P_i, R[j,i])
```

and verifies that the second 806-entry coefficient vector combines their
factor-base exponents to eight times `(-2, -1, -1)`. Therefore it proves,
without expanding an astronomically large field element,

```text
G_1^8 = principal(product(alpha_j, c_j) * Ge_1^-8).
```

This is the exact factored principal witness. The same replay proves the
order-24 source generator (whose direct reduction has an empty scaling
factorization). Results atomically normalize the complete generator tuples to
the public `[8, 24]` order; each tuple retains its source index. The checker
reports two signed reductions, two principal order witnesses, 806 replayed raw
principal equations, and 4,962 exact ideal products. Mutating either published
generator, either class-witness coefficient block, a source factor-base ideal,
or a correspondence/metadata authority is rejected. It also constructs the
opposite principal-correction sign and proves that false equation is rejected.
