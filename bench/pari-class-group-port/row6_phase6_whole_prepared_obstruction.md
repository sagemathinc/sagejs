source-transparent native graph. The generated

## Remaining whole-prepared obstruction

This is still **not** a whole prepared class-and-unit kernel. The terminal
native graph remains a separate invocation. That terminal graph already
joins analytic work, post-HNF acceptance, Smith invariants, rank-two units,
exact factor-base reconstruction, all 1,137 principal equations, and
class-witness projection, but its inputs are still allocated and populated
after the connected Gate-C/ancestry root returns.

The remaining closure task is therefore specific: join the retained outputs
of `row6_phase6_gate_prefix_root.generated.py` directly to the existing fused
terminal class/unit graph, keep terminal publication storage resident, and
preserve the same transactional precision and capacity failures. Until that
last native-call boundary is removed, timing remains disabled; timing either
graph separately would measure a surrogate rather than the complete prepared
class-and-unit computation.
