# Row-14 prepared Gate-C audit

## Result

Gate C is connected to the immutable prepared initial-root owner. A capped
worker accepted only the authenticated neutral prepared-number-field
projection and the content-addressed Gate-A/B owner, hydrated the resident
collector, and completed the authentic `42 -> 802 -> 804 -> 805 -> 806`
collection/HNF schedule.

The worker did not receive the historical capsule, W0 trace, factor-metadata
receipt, or the previously accepted 806-relation owner. After it exited, the
checker admitted W0 as a cold differential oracle and matched every relation,
exact log embedding, HNF block, dependency block, basis block, transformed log
block, and permutation at all four HNF checkpoints. It also matched the final
806 relation multipliers, hashes, origins, and automorphism identities.

The qualifying run took `90,906,561,050 ns` inside the connected host. Its
peak RSS was `1,227,312 KiB`, and its conservative simultaneous owned-buffer
upper bound was `1,552,832,864` bytes.

## Boundary and schedule

The positive prepared projection is the same 23-field allowlist used by the
prepared initial-root checker. The host rejects extra prepared fields, verifies
the prepared authority against the root owner, and verifies the exact Gate-B
state before collection. The root owner has plain SHA-256
`b2088268bca4b735816d280d15bccb94f45faab9831bc7523c9dc671b6716ae0`
and is required to be read-only.

The seven continuation passes requested `[4,2,1,1,1,1,1]` relations. They
produced `[804,805,805,805,805,805,806]`; the five single-relation attempts
include four authentic retries before the final admission. Exact schedule and
outer-control vectors are asserted for every pass. The resident HNF states are:

```text
802  [3,10,792,4,7,105,0,802,0]
804  [4,11,793,2,7,1,0,804,0]
805  [2,9,796,1,7,3,0,805,0]
806  [3,10,796,0,7,0,0,806,0]
```

Forbidden mutations to capsule descriptors, initial relations, bounds, and RNG
state leave the neutral prepared projection unchanged. This demonstrates that
those answer-bearing values cannot cross the worker ABI.

## Capacity and scope

The worker runs under a 4-GiB address-space limit, a 600-second CPU limit, a
600-second wall timeout, and a 3-GiB V8 old-space ceiling. The checker records
both process peak RSS and the conservative owned-buffer upper bound and rejects
either at 4 GiB. Logical collection capacity remains the public Gate-B owner
capacity of 8,110 records; no oracle-derived allocation size is admitted.

This audit proves the prepared-root-to-accepted-HNF connection only. It does
not claim Gate D terminal class computation, units, a matched PARI timing, or a
general prepared-number-field implementation.
