# Unit maps after durable class/unit resumption

The bounded `ClassUnitComputation.unit_coordinate_map()` interface also accepts
standard generic conditional terminal results whose checkpoint or callback
controls exclude uninterrupted live reuse. It does not change the context's
`reusable` flag. Ordinary uninterrupted results retain their existing live
authentication path.

For the nonreusable path, map construction:

1. Copies bounded component and analytic **claims**, without invoking producer
   verification callbacks or rediscovering units.
2. Runs the existing detached completion receiver once: fresh maximal order,
   exact prime ideals and relations, presentation, compact unit membership,
   complete torsion, justified generating base, and fresh BF analytic index one.
3. Copies the verified ordered compact basis and torsion into the original field
   after checking identical defining polynomial, maximal-order basis and proof
   assumptions. It retains no producer-owned basis as its arithmetic authority.
4. Checks source identities, source proof/basis/torsion semantics, retained
   factor-base/relation/presentation claims and the owned map data before each
   map operation. A changed source fails closed; a map
   operation does not rerun completion or silently re-admit a changed source.

The public detached export functions retain their existing live-source
eligibility. The internal serialization path is deliberately separate from
mathematical admission. No public constructor accepts a report dictionary,
restored token, verifier callback or `trusted` flag. Hashes bind data, not truth.

## Mathematical boundary

Exact principal relations over a generating factor base give a finite relation
quotient of order `h'` with `h` dividing `h'`. Exact compact units and complete
torsion give a unit subgroup `U'`. The existing fresh BF calculation bounds the
positive integer product `(h'/h) [O_K^*:U']`. An index-one result proves both
indices are one under the explicitly recorded hypotheses. Full rank alone,
component replay alone, or a numerical regulator does not establish a
fundamental unit basis.

The BDF completion envelope retains both class-character GRH and BF zeta GRH;
the Minkowski envelope retains its existing BF hypothesis. Map admission requires
agreement with the source's declared proof policy, and never upgrades a
conditional result to unconditional. Existing generation and analytic owners
remain the sole mathematical implementation; map admission adds no new theorem.

The original and freshly reconstructed fields have the identical presentation.
Copying exact power-basis coordinates is therefore the canonical transport,
not an arbitrary number-field isomorphism search. Coordinates refer to the
verified ordered basis, with torsion first.

## Limits and intentional declines

All detached component, generation, analytic and coordinate resource limits are
unchanged. In particular the complete component envelope supports degrees two
through four, at most 32 factor-base ideals and 128 relations. Map construction
may decline a completed computation outside that bounded replay envelope.
Admission replay is additional work, not a free cache hit or a competitive
performance claim.

The source certificate retained for mutation detection must also fit the
existing 4 MiB byte ceiling. This private snapshot restriction does not change
the public compact export's eligibility. Before copying, the private path bounds
dimensions, sparse rows, scalar bit lengths, container breadth/depth and a
conservative aggregate JSON size. Copied map data is also checked before
snapshot allocation; torsion and compact factors must belong to the original
field. Diagnostic relation provenance and
discarded approximate logs are not part of the mathematical claim snapshot.

Ranks above three, general positive-rank complex torsion coordinates, and
arbitrary detached factored membership remain unsupported. `factored_exp`
constructs compact products; `exp` explicitly expands only within the existing
weighted arithmetic-size limit. Large compact products do not authorize an
expansion-limit increase. Unsupported cases do not fall back to producer
callbacks, fresh class/unit discovery or weaker proof assumptions.

The focused regression uses durable nonempty prefixes for a real cubic with
class invariants `(2,2)` and a mixed quartic with class invariants `(2)`. It checks
fresh-process resumption, original-field compact maps, no producer callbacks or
expansion, single admission replay, source/map mutation rejection and live
rank-three preservation. Existing detached replay subgroup counterexamples are
still required. These tests exercise the argument; they do not formally prove it.

Broad integration, source/resource inventory review and native/platform
qualification remain separate release gates.
